Install in four steps

Your phone asks for permission once because the app isn’t from the Play Store. That’s normal.

  1. Download. Open this page in Chrome and tap Download APK. If a warning appears, choose Download anyway.
  2. Allow installs. Open the file. If your phone says “Install unknown apps”, tap Settings, allow Chrome, then go back.
  3. Install. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, choose Install anyway.
  4. Open LYRA. Allow access to music and videos and your library fills itself.

Questions

Why does Play Protect show a warning?

LYRA doesn’t come from the Play Store, so Google flags it as an unknown source. The app only asks for access to your music, videos and notifications. Install it only if you’re comfortable with that.

Does LYRA download videos or songs from the internet?

No. LYRA is a player for files already on your phone. It has no online downloader.

Where do the ads come from?

Ads come from Google AdMob and only appear when you choose to unlock the extras, never in the middle of a song or video. In the EU and UK you will see Google’s consent form first.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Your songs, videos and playlists stay on your phone, and LYRA doesn’t ask you to sign in.

How do I update?

Download the newest APK from this page and install it over the old one. Your playlists and favorites stay.
